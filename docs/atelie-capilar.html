02 / Manifesto

Hair as territory.

Architecture is a method of observing relationships: mass, scale, direction, material and spatial composition. Ateliê Capilar explores these relationships through hair, without reducing human identity to formulas. The goal is a space for creating, comparing and reflecting on possibilities.
