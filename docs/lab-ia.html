02 / Initial experiment

A small Transformer, a real research question.

The initial model is a small causal Transformer implemented in PyTorch, trained from randomly initialized weights with character-level tokenization. The purpose is to understand the training process, not to establish professional competence. A reduction in training loss alone does not demonstrate generalization or factual accuracy.

Interactive concept / causal attention

Choose a token position to see which positions it is allowed to attend to in a causal attention mask. Future tokens are masked.

Didactic illustration of permitted attention positions. No live model, trained weights, attention scores or inference are used.
